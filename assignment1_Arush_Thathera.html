Welcome to St.Francis University

St.Francis University:is a small, close-knit college where every student is known by name. Since our founding in 1962, we have helped more than 20,000 graduates turn their curiosity into careers that matter to their communities. Our approach is simple: small classes, real projects, and teachers who have time for you. Whether you want to write code, care for patients, or protect the environment, you will learn by doing, not just by listening.

Our Mission: We prepare thoughtful, capable graduates who use what they learn to serve others. We believe education works best when it is practical, honest, and kind.

Why Choose Us?Our classes average just 18 students, and every undergraduate completes at least one internship or community project before graduation. Our B.Sc. and B.A. programs are built with local employers, so what you study is what the workplace needs. Applications for the next intake open every January.
